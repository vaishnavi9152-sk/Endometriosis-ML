Abdominal Bloating ("Endo Belly")
Endometriosis-associated bloating — severe abdominal distension, often causing visible swelling

Mittelschmerz (Ovulation Pain / Mid-Cycle Pain)
Mittelschmerz — pain occurring mid-cycle (approximately 14 days before menstruation); highly specific indicator of endometriosis
Do you get pain in your lower belly about 2 weeks before your period starts?
Referred Lumbar & Sciatic Pain (Back / Leg Pain)
Referred pain — pain radiating to lower back, buttocks, or legs due to endometriosis pressing on pelvic nerves
During your period, does pain travel to your lower back, buttocks, or down your legs?
Nausea & Gastrointestinal Symptoms
Endometriosis-associated nausea — GI symptoms including nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea during menstruation
Do you feel sick, nauseous, or have stomach problems around your period?
